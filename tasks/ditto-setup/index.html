Set up the Ditto mail sorter

Ditto keeps junk and advertisements out of your inbox. It runs on Mike's computer. To connect it, your mail provider gives you a special password for Ditto only, and you send it to Mike. It takes about 10 minutes. Please use a computer, not a phone.

I use Gmail I use Yahoo

Before you start

  • This special password is called an app password. It has 16 letters.
  • It only lets Ditto read and sort your mail. It cannot change your normal password or see anything else in your account.
  • Your normal password does not change. Keep signing in the way you always do.
  • You can switch Ditto off at any time: delete the app password named ditto (the last step in each section shows where).

Gmail

Part 1: Turn on 2-Step Verification

Google gives app passwords only to accounts with 2-Step Verification. If yours already has it, go to Part 2.

Open Google 2-Step Verification

If Google asks you to sign in, sign in. You then land on the right page.

  1. Click Turn on 2-Step Verification.
  2. Follow the steps: Google texts a code to your phone, and you type it in.
  3. When the page says 2-Step Verification is On, go to Part 2.

Part 2: Make the app password

Open Google App passwords

Google may ask for your password again. Type it, and you land on the right page.

  1. In the box App name, type ditto and click Create.
  2. Google shows a password of 16 letters in a yellow box, in four groups of four.
  3. Do Part 3 now. Google shows this password only once.
If the app passwords page says "The setting you are looking for is not available for your account", 2-Step Verification is not on yet (go back to Part 1). If it still says that, your account is a work or school account: tell Mike.

Part 3: Send it to Mike

  1. Send Mike a text message to the number he gave you, with two lines:
    your Gmail address
    the 16 letters (spaces do not matter)
  2. Then click Done on the Google page.

To switch Ditto off later: myaccount.google.com/apppasswords → click the trash can next to ditto.

Yahoo

Part 1: Turn on two-step verification

Yahoo requires this first. If yours already has it, go to Part 2.

Open Yahoo Account security

If Yahoo asks you to sign in, sign in. You then land on the right page.

  1. Find Two-step verification and click Turn on.
  2. Choose Phone number, type your mobile number, and click Send code.
  3. Type the code Yahoo texts you and click Verify.

Part 2: Make the app password

Open Yahoo Account security

The same page as Part 1. If you are still on it, just scroll.

  1. Find App passwords (it may say "Generate and manage app passwords") and click it.
  2. In the app name box, type ditto.
  3. Click Generate password. Yahoo shows a password of 16 letters.

Part 3: Send it to Mike

  1. Send Mike a text message to the number he gave you, with two lines:
    your Yahoo address
    the 16 letters (spaces do not matter)
  2. Then click Done on the Yahoo page.

To switch Ditto off later: login.yahoo.com/account/security → App passwords → remove ditto.

After you send it

Mike connects your account. Within a few minutes two new folders appear in your mail: _ditto junk quarantine and _ditto marketing quarantine.

  • Drag junk messages into _ditto junk quarantine.
  • Drag advertisements you do not want into _ditto marketing quarantine.

You can drag (or move) messages from your phone's mail app or from a computer, whichever you use. Only the setup above needs a computer.

After you do this a few times, look-alike mail goes there by itself. Ditto starts after 3 messages of each kind, and it gets better with every message you drag after that, so keep dragging the ones it misses. Junk goes to spam after a day; advertisements go to the trash after a week. If Ditto moves something you want, drag it back to your inbox, and it learns from that too. Once a week you get a short summary email.

If anything on your screen looks different from these steps, text Mike a photo of the screen.

Tip: put the two Ditto folders at the top of your folder list, so dragging is easy

New folders often appear at the bottom of your folder list, so you have to scroll to drag a message there. Move them up once:

  • iPhone or iPad Mail: on the Mailboxes screen tap Edit, tick the two Ditto folders, then drag them up by the three lines on the right. Tap Done.
  • Mail on a Mac: drag each Ditto folder into Favorites in the sidebar.
  • Other mail apps: if you can drag folders, drag them to the top. If not, look for Favorites or a star next to the folder.
