Set up the Ditto mail sorter

Ditto keeps junk and advertisements out of your inbox. It runs on Mike's computer. To connect it, your mail provider gives you a special password for Ditto only, and you send it to Mike. It takes about 3 minutes, on your phone or a computer.

I use Gmail I use Yahoo

Before you start

  • This special password is called an app password. It has 16 letters.
  • It only lets Ditto read and sort your mail. It cannot change your normal password or see anything else in your account.
  • Your normal password does not change. Keep signing in the way you always do.
  • Keep this page open. Each blue button opens Gmail or Yahoo in a new tab, so this page stays in its own tab. After each step, click this page's tab at the top of the browser to come back here.
  • You can switch Ditto off at any time: text Mike ditto off. To remove Ditto completely, delete the app password named ditto (the end of each section shows where).

Gmail

Each blue button below opens Google in a new tab. When you finish there, come back to this tab for the next step.

Step 1: Make the app password

Open Google: App passwords (new tab)

Google asks you to sign in, then shows the App passwords page.

  1. In the box App name, type ditto and click Create.
  2. Google shows a password of 16 letters in a yellow box, in four groups of four.
  3. Do Step 2 now. Google shows this password only once.
If the page says "The setting you are looking for is not available for your account", Google wants 2-Step Verification on first. Click this button, then click Turn on 2-Step Verification and follow the steps: Google texts a code to your phone, and you type it in. Open Google 2-Step Verification (new tab) When the page says 2-Step Verification is On, go back to Step 1 and click its blue button again. If it still says the setting is not available, your account is a work or school account: tell Mike.

Step 2: Send it to Mike

  1. Send Mike a text message to the number he gave you. Put each of these three things on its own line: 
    ditto
your Gmail address
the 16 letters
    Type your own address and letters where the grey words are.
    Line 1 is the word ditto, so Mike's agent can connect your account. Spaces in the 16 letters do not matter.
  2. Then click Done on the Google page.

To switch Ditto off or back on, or to ask how it is doing: text Mike a message that starts with the word ditto, for example ditto off, ditto on or ditto status. You can also text any other question about Ditto: make sure the word ditto is in your message, and Mike's agent will try to deal with it.

To remove Ditto completely: open myaccount.google.com/apppasswords and click the trash can next to ditto. Ditto then cannot read your mail at all.

Yahoo

Each blue button below opens Yahoo in a new tab. When you finish there, come back to this tab for the next step.

Step 1: Make the app password

Open Yahoo: Generate app password (new tab)

Yahoo asks you to sign in, then shows the "Generate app password" box.

  1. In the box, type ditto.
  2. Click Generate password. Yahoo shows a password of 16 letters.
  3. Do Step 2 now.
If Yahoo says you must turn on two-step verification first: click this button, then click Turn on, choose Phone number, type your mobile number, click Send code, type the code Yahoo texts you and click Verify. Open Yahoo Two-step verification (new tab) Then go back to Step 1 and click its blue button again.

Step 2: Send it to Mike

  1. Send Mike a text message to the number he gave you. Put each of these three things on its own line: 
    ditto
your Yahoo address
the 16 letters
    Type your own address and letters where the grey words are.
    Line 1 is the word ditto, so Mike's agent can connect your account. Spaces in the 16 letters do not matter.
  2. Then click Done on the Yahoo page.

To switch Ditto off or back on, or to ask how it is doing: text Mike a message that starts with the word ditto, for example ditto off, ditto on or ditto status. You can also text any other question about Ditto: make sure the word ditto is in your message, and Mike's agent will try to deal with it.

To remove Ditto completely: open login.yahoo.com/myaccount/security/app-password and remove ditto. Ditto then cannot read your mail at all.

After you send it

Mike's agent connects your account automatically and texts you back. In about 15 minutes two new folders appear in your mail: _ditto junk quarantine and _ditto marketing quarantine.

  • Drag junk messages into _ditto junk quarantine.
  • Drag advertisements you do not want into _ditto marketing quarantine.

You can drag (or move) messages from your phone's mail app or from a computer, whichever you use. Only the setup above needs a computer.

After you do this a few times, look-alike mail goes there by itself. Ditto starts after 3 messages of each kind, and it gets better with every message you drag after that, so keep dragging the ones it misses. Junk goes to spam after a day; advertisements go to the trash after a week. If Ditto moves something you want, drag it back to your inbox, and it learns from that too. Once a week you get a short summary email.

If anything on your screen looks different from these steps, text Mike a photo of the screen.

Tip: put the two Ditto folders at the top of your folder list, so dragging is easy

New folders often appear at the bottom of your folder list, so you have to scroll to drag a message there. Move them up once:

  • iPhone or iPad Mail: on the Mailboxes screen tap Edit, tick the two Ditto folders, then drag them up by the three lines on the right. Tap Done.
  • Mail on a Mac: drag each Ditto folder into Favorites in the sidebar.
  • Other mail apps: if you can drag folders, drag them to the top. If not, look for Favorites or a star next to the folder.
