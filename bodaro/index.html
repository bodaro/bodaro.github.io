Bodaro

Coming soon.

Bodaro is a personal mail-organizing tool. It runs on its owner's own computer and works on the owner's own mailboxes. It is not a public service and it has no other users.

What it does

Bodaro watches a mailbox and files mail the way its owner already files mail by hand. The owner moves a message into a folder; Bodaro learns from that move and files later mail from the same sender the same way. It also sets junk and marketing mail aside in holding folders so the owner can review it.
